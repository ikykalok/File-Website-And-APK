QUESTIONS

Straight answers

Is Echo Music really free?

+

Yes, completely. Echo has no paid tiers, no ads, and no subscription. It's open-source software supported by the community.

Where do I download Echo?

+

Echo is distributed through GitHub Releases. Tap the Download button above to go directly to the latest APK.

Does Echo need an account?

+

No account, no sign-up, no tracking. Open the app and play.

What Android version do I need?

+

Echo runs on Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) and above.

Can I contribute to Echo?

+

Absolutely. Head to github.com/EchoMusicApp/Echo-Music to open issues, submit PRs, or suggest features. All contributors are welcome.

Where does the music come from?

+

Echo Music is a third-party YouTube Music client. It provides a customized app experience, but Echo Music itself does not host, own, or hold any of the songs.
